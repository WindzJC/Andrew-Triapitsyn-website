The Author

A writer of mechanisms, not symptoms.

Andrew Triapitsyn writes about the systems that shape perception: psychological, technological, economic and political. His work focuses on the mechanisms behind manipulation rather than its symptoms, and on what independent judgment costs the people who keep it. He lives in Ireland.

Alongside his published nonfiction, he is developing several series in both fiction and nonfiction. They will be listed in the catalogue below as each is announced.

Immunity to Illusions

“This is not a book about other people’s gullibility.”

From the book’s description

A systematic investigation into how modern environments shape perception, not mainly through censorship but through design: platforms, institutions, incentives and the vocabulary we are given to think with.

It applies the same scrutiny to every side, including the reader’s own. What it offers in return is not a new allegiance, but a sharper and more independent judgment.

Inspect the frame

Question what shapes what you see.

An interactive diagram of the book’s method of examination: an interpretation, not an excerpt. Move inward one layer at a time.

Choose a layer to inspect

Layer 1 of 5 · Surface

What appears: a feed, a headline, a choice that feels like your own.

Ask What is being shown, and what has been left outside the frame?

Layer 2 of 5 · Design

How it is arranged: defaults, sequences and friction that make some choices effortless and others unnatural.

Ask What does this make easy, and what does it make hard?

Layer 3 of 5 · Incentive

Who benefits: attention, dependence and revenue that reward the arrangement.

Ask Who gains if I believe this, click this, or stay?

Layer 4 of 5 · Language

What can be said: a vocabulary that quietly narrows the questions it is possible to ask.

Ask Which words am I given, and which questions do they rule out?

Layer 5 of 5 · Yourself

The layer scrutiny most often skips: what you already want to be true.

Ask Would I accept this argument if it favoured the other side?
